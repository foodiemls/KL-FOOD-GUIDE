🍨 Gelulu Gelato

📍 Jalan Tun H S Lee, Pasar Seni
💰 RM10-20/person
🍨 Dessert
🚆 5 minutes walk from LRT station
🍦 Dessert
❤️ Chill Place

⭐ Honest Review

🍽 Food: 7/10
💰 Value: 6/10
🌿 Environment: 7/10

🍦 Must Try

  • Pistachio Gelato
  • Peanut Butter Gelato

The gelato is not overly sweet and has unique flavours that do not taste artificial.

The environment is cosy with air-conditioning and a quality sound system playing relaxing music.

👥 Suitable For

Friends gathering, chit-chatting, and couple dessert dates.

🕒 Opening Hours

Daily: 12PM - 10:30PM
Friday & Saturday: 12PM - 1AM

🔄 Would I Come Back?

✅ Yes. They update flavours regularly, including special flavours like Pu'er and Thai Tea.

🥭 XIAO BY NHX

📍 Jalan Petaling, Pasar Seni
💰 RM10-20/person
🍨 Dessert
🚆 Right outside Pasar Seni LRT Station
🥭 Dessert
🚆 Convenient Location

⭐ Honest Review

🍽 Food: 8/10
💰 Value: 6/10
🌿 Environment: 6/10

🍮 Must Try

  • Mango Pomelo Nectar
  • Taro Mochi Cloud Milk

The desserts are not overly sweet, with layered textures and authentic ingredients.

The shop is air-conditioned but has limited seating. More suitable for takeaway or a quick rest.

👥 Suitable For

A short break after walking around Pasar Seni, or grabbing a drink while exploring the area.

🕒 Opening Hours

Daily: 12PM - 10PM

🔄 Would I Come Back?

✅ Yes. The milk tea has a nice aroma and good texture.
